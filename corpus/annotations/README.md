# Lynceus annotations

**English** · [Français](README.fr.md)

Hand readings of the pages in the evaluation set, against which every Lynceus analysis
chain is measured. How they are made: [docs/en/ANNOTATION.md](../../docs/en/ANNOTATION.md).

## Joining

1. Read [docs/en/ANNOTATION.md](../../docs/en/ANNOTATION.md), and in particular its rules.
2. Open a pull request that adds your line to the table above, in alphabetical
   order. Sign your commit (`git commit -s`): under the
   [Developer Certificate of Origin](../../DCO.txt), you thereby certify that you have the
   right to publish your readings under the licence of this folder. That is your agreement.
3. The coordination then sends you your first pages. Your readings are published once the
   pages are closed, and each publishing commit credits you as co-author.
